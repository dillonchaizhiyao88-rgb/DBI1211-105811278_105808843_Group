Borneo Cultures Museum

The second-biggest museum in Southeast Asia, the Borneo Cultures Museum is a historic establishment. It is a prominent destination for discovering Borneo's rich natural and cultural history, housed in an eye-catching, contemporary structure with a unique golden roof. The immersive, multi-story layout and cutting-edge interactive multimedia displays that appeal to guests of all ages are often praised by reviewers.

DBKU Cat Museum

The Kuching North City Hall (DBKU) offices is home to the unusual and distinctive DBKU Cat Museum. Honoring the city's nickname (because "Kuching" means "cat" in Malay), this eccentric location showcases thousands of cat-related artworks, historical displays, images, and mementos from all over the world.

The cat museum is well-liked by cat fans.

Fort Margherita (The Brooke Gallery)

Located on the banks of the Sarawak River, the superbly renovated 1879 English-style fort is home to the Brooke Gallery at Fort Margherita. The "White Rajahs"—the Brooke family dynasty that dominated Sarawak for more than a century—are the main subject of the exhibition, which also highlights the historical interactions between the locals and British officials.

It is no longer accessible to tourists. However, the landscape is still well-liked.
